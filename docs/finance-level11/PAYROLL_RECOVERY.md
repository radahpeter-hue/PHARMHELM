# Payroll recovery and submission safeguards

Base: 543177c. Draft candidate; no live records modified.

- HR distinguishes approved outstanding, recorded Level 11 payments, pending Finance, returned, and unconfirmed legacy payroll. Legacy automatic paid labels are not payment evidence.
- Rejected legacy salary requests appear in HR. Recovery requires the operator to review dates and payment evidence; it retains the rejected expense unchanged and anchors the replacement batch ID permanently to the source request ID; a different date selection cannot create a second replacement. The original expense is retained unchanged. Requests with recorded payment evidence cannot use this recovery path. Historical records do not contain reliable original date ranges, so HR must explicitly select them.
- Recorded and verified attendance hours are shown separately. No expected-hours fallback or automatic verification was introduced. Existing Attendance verification remains the authorized review path. Generation waits for attendance loading.
- Payroll dates are locked while a draft is open. Kampala month defaults preserve the local calendar date. Submit appears below the employee table and opens a cancelable summary confirmation; an immediate in-memory lock prevents double-click submissions.
- The service rejects overlapping submitted/returned/approved/paid batches before staging. Exact-period retries and returned same-ID revisions remain resumable.
- New batches reserve each employee/calendar day through immutable documents. A reservation's predecessor proves contiguous coverage from the batch start. Employee staging requires the final day's reservation. Concurrent different period IDs cannot reserve the same employee/day. Reservations persist through returns and incomplete submissions: resume or correct the owning batch rather than starting another overlapping batch. No release/delete operation is provided.
- Each employee’s full date reservation and employee staging commit atomically; monthly payroll adds roughly one reservation write per employee/day. A losing concurrent reservation leaves no partially owned dates. Empty first-revision batches with no staged employees do not block otherwise unpaid dates. Existing staged reservations remain protected. Plan the Spark daily write budget for large or unusually long payroll periods.
- Existing Level 11 batches retain their existing revision/payment path. Their historical dates are checked by the service/UI; the new reservation proof is mandatory for newly created batches. Pre-upgrade records remain subject to legacy reconciliation, not automatic inferred payments or mass migration.

Historical cost reconciliation candidate (25 November): HR explicitly selects the legacy employee payroll entries belonging to the rejected source. The service checks their tenant, period, absence of payment evidence and net total against the original rejected Finance request; another recovery cannot reuse linked entries through this service. The selection is immutable across revisions. Finance sees those entries and must explicitly confirm reconciliation before approval, with the selection and confirmation retained in its immutable approval event. Pending/returned recoveries preserve historical cost. Approved/partial/paid replacements exclude only their explicitly linked legacy entries from P&L and statutory reports, preventing duplicate recognition while retaining the old rows. This is human reconciliation, not proof that old paid labels represent bank or cash settlement; ambiguous/mismatched histories remain blocked for Finance review.
